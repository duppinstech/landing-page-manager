When the Battery Isn't Under the Hood

Manufacturers have been moving batteries out of the engine bay for years. Under the rear seat. In the trunk. Behind panels. Some vehicles use AGM batteries that need the car's computer told a new battery is installed, or the charging system will overcharge and shorten its life.

Charmeck has 20 years working with these exact situations. Escalades and Tahoes with underseat mounts. European models requiring a scan tool to complete the install. We bring the tools, do the coding, and leave your vehicle running the way it should.

Common Questions

What to Expect

Do I need a jumpstart or a battery replacement? +
We test your battery and charging system when we arrive. If the battery holds a charge after a jump start and the alternator is working correctly, a replacement may not be necessary. If the battery is failing or below spec, we will tell you and have a replacement available on-site.
Why does my European car need battery registration? +
BMW, Audi, Mercedes, and similar vehicles use battery management systems that monitor charging cycles. When a new battery is installed, the system must be told a new battery is present. Without registration, the car's computer continues charging at old-battery parameters, which can shorten the new battery's life significantly. We handle this with a scan tool as part of the install.
What does the 42-month warranty cover? +
The warranty covers battery defects and premature failure under normal use for 42 months from the date of purchase. For specific warranty terms, ask when you call and we will walk you through what is covered.
How long will it take for you to arrive? +
Our target is 30 minutes or less within our service area. Actual arrival times depend on your location and current demand. Call us at 704-642-9432 and we will give you a realistic estimate before you wait.
What are your service hours? +
We operate 9 AM to 5 PM. If you are calling outside those hours, leave a voicemail and we will return your call as soon as we are back in service.
